## Approved method

1. Inspect the source package and existing document in detail. Verify provenance, coordinate reference system, geometry validity, area and spatial relationships before map production.
2. Use clear georeferenced imagery and traceable GIS layers. Do not substitute generated illustrations for factual maps.
3. Preserve the approved A3 landscape composition (420 × 297 mm): primary map on the left; title, legend, scale, orientation, sources and inset on the right. Use the actual approved template for exact dimensions. Adapt extent and thematic content to the evidence.
4. Keep the application polygon distinct from engineering work limits, material destinations and assessed influence areas.
5. Integrate relevant map excerpts into the narrative and complete A3 sheets into the annex. Synchronize discussion, captions, source notes, figure/map lists, numbering and annex references.
6. For documents governed by this approved format: Letter narrative pages, Calibri 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing, 2.54 cm margins; tables 10 pt and captions 9 pt. Preserve project-specific explicit exceptions.
7. Render the document and inspect every page. Check table continuation, clipped or missing rows, orphaned paragraphs, image readability, page sizes, references and completeness. Repair the specific defect and verify again.
8. Save the verified revision with its artifact identity and change history. Preserve source records and production dependencies required to reproduce it.

## Evidence boundaries

- Regional bathymetry is not a local sounding survey, navigation product or dredging design.
- A limited habitat dataset is not a complete local habitat inventory.
- A spatial-plan status sheet is not a completed official zoning overlay.
- A review framework is not an approved engineering design.
- Geometric screening buffers are not automatically setbacks or impact boundaries.
- User approval of presentation and workflow does not validate unresolved technical or regulatory conclusions.

## Continuity rule

Reuse the approved method and visual standard for the next relevant project; independently verify each project's data, extent, scale and interpretation. A model, tool or runtime change must not silently revert to an earlier rejected layout or lower the evidence standard.

Change this approved standard only under an explicit applicable instruction from Luqman. Keep ZEROCODE terminology out of client-facing professional documents.
